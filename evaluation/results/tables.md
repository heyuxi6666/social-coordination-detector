Generated by `summarize.py` from `raw_results.csv`: 44 configurations x 20 seeds (base seed 1000), 880 synthetic datasets, Python 3.11.9. All numbers are measured on synthetic data only.

Reading guide: *Detected* = share of coordinated datasets where some predicted group matches the true coordinated posts (Jaccard >= 0.5), with a Wilson 95% interval. *P/R/F1* = account level, counts pooled over seeds; *F1 (mean ± SE)* = per-seed F1. *Spurious* = predicted groups that do not match, per dataset. *Acct FPR* = flagged non-coordinated accounts / all non-coordinated accounts seen. *Organic FP* = share of organic-only datasets with at least one predicted group. Runtime is the median wall-clock for one dataset (30 posts) and varies by machine.
